 I am a soon-to-be PhD student at  [Science Tokyo](https://www.isct.ac.jp/en)(formly TokyoTech) under the supervision of Prof.[Takafumi Kanamori](https://sites.google.com/site/tokyotechkanamoritakafumilab/english).  My research focuses on the integration of machine learning and algorithm design, exploring areas such as [algorithms with predictions](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2006.09123), [data-driven algorithm design](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.07177), and [ML for combinatorial optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.06128). Before stepping into academic research, competitive programming was a big part of my life([AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp/users/Gosu_Hiroo), [codeforces](https://codeforces.com/profile/gosu_hiroo)), which shaped my current research interest. 

I love puzzle games and online competitive games - Dota 2 and StarCraft II being two of my all-time favorites. This interest extends to game research, including game AI and design principles, though I haven't had the opportunity to explore these areas extensively. Previously, I've also explored reinforcement learning in my research, lured by projects like [AlphaStar](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/alphastar-mastering-the-real-time-strategy-game-starcraft-ii/) and [OpenAI Five](https://openai.com/index/openai-five/).

In October 2024, I discovered the joys of indoor climbing, which quickly became my favorite sports. It's not only incredibly fun but also a great way to improve my concentration and boost my productivity throughout the day. On other days, I enjoy reading mathematics books, especially those [recommended](https://www.3blue1brown.com/blog/book-recommendations) by [3B1B](https://www.youtube.com/c/3blue1brown).
